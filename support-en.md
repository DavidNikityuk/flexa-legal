# Flexa Support

*Українська версія: [Підтримка](../support/)*

Write to us — we reply within a day: **support.flexa@gmail.com**

To help us respond faster, tell us which account you use (trainer or client), what happened and on which screen.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your data is deleted together with the account.

**How do I report another user?** In the chat, tap the other person's name or long-press a message → "Report". Or write to us at the address above.

**Who sees my health data?** Only you — until you turn on "Share with trainer" in the settings. More in the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-en/).

**How do I get a copy of my data?** Write to us at the address above — we will send it within 30 days.
